HomeCall Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Zero-Login & Anonymity

HomeCall does not collect, store, or require any personally identifiable information (PII). No phone numbers, email addresses, names, contacts, or passwords are ever requested or stored. Each user is assigned an anonymous, random session identifier (e.g. User-XXXXXX) generated locally on their device.

2. Microphone & Audio Usage

The microphone permission (RECORD_AUDIO) is used exclusively for live, real-time voice communication during active voice calls. Voice audio is streamed directly in real-time via Agora RTC SDK and WebRTC with hardware noise suppression, acoustic echo cancellation, and auto-gain control. Voice data is never recorded, never stored on any server, and never shared with any third party.

3. Real-Time Signaling & Presence

Firebase Realtime Database is used strictly for call signaling (notifying when an incoming call arrives and exchanging audio session parameters) and temporary presence (online/offline status). Signal data is automatically removed when a call ends or when the user goes offline.

4. Background Service & Notifications

The app uses a foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC) and notification permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) to maintain continuous reachability for incoming calls when the device is locked or minimized, displaying a high-priority incoming call banner with ringtone.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via the official project repository: https://github.com/mrdkpandey2010-eng/homecall
